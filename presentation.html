Problem & Motivation

Campus Life Fragmentation vs Centralized Hub

Why 5,000+ MITSians needed a single authoritative digital home for student activities.

The Traditional Campus Chaos

  • Scattered WhatsApp Groups: Vital recruitment deadlines & announcements get buried under thousands of chat messages.
  • Unverified Registrations: Anyone could create fake posters or solicit registration fees without college authorization.
  • Zero Faculty Oversight: Coordinators had no dashboard to monitor active members, fees, or event schedules.
  • Lost Club Knowledge: When seniors graduate, club social handles, assets, and records disappear into thin air.

The MITS Club Hub Solution

  • Strict College Authentication: Only verified @mitsgwl.ac.in Google emails can access the portal.
  • Two-Factor Phone SMS Security: Presidents and core members must verify physical mobile numbers with real OTP.
  • Official Professor Approvals Queue: Every club is audited by a designated faculty mentor before going public.
  • Permanent Digital Legacy: Complete historical archive of leaders, past galleries, and active rosters.

Modern Serverless Cloud Architecture

Full-Stack Technical Foundation

Two-Factor Authentication Speaker 1: Akash Dhakad

Real Telecom SMS 2FA Phone Verification

Physical SIM authentication protecting student leadership and direct contact channels.

Engineering Real SMS Delivery

  • E.164 Automated Formatter: Converts raw 10-digit Indian mobile inputs into international standard (+91XXXXXXXXXX).
  • Invisible Google reCAPTCHA: Background verification algorithm blocks bots without annoying image puzzles.
  • Dynamic Carrier Dispatch: Leverages Google Cloud telecom routing to deliver real 6-digit SMS OTPs directly to Airtel, Jio, Vi, and BSNL numbers.
  • Auto-Recovery Mechanism: Handles reCAPTCHA DOM reuse gracefully, ensuring zero runtime exceptions for the user.
  • Firestore Verified Badge: Successful confirmations permanently unlock the verified 2FA Verified leadership badge.

User Manual

Three-Tier User Operational Manual

Clear operational instructions for Students, Club Leaders, and Faculty Mentors.

For MITS Students

  • Step 1: Click Login with College Email using your @mitsgwl.ac.in account.
  • Step 2: Use the 3D Filter Console to browse clubs by category, fee, and recruitment status.
  • Step 3: Open any club, inspect "Kya Hai, Kyu Hai", and tap Join Club / Apply Now.
  • Step 4: Track your application status under My Applications.

For Club Presidents

  • Step 1: Tap + Register Official Club and enter your society details and gallery photos.
  • Step 2: Input your designated Coordinator Professor's official email address.
  • Step 3: Use the 1-Click Gmail dispatcher to send the formal review letter to your mentor.
  • Step 4: Once authorized, open Manage Applicants to review candidate submissions.

For Faculty Mentors

  • Step 1: Sign in with your official faculty @mitsgwl.ac.in credentials.
  • Step 2: The Official Professor Approvals Queue will highlight all clubs assigned to you.
  • Step 3: Review the club description, leadership contact, and proposed membership fee.
  • Step 4: Add remarks and click Authorize & Publish to launch the club.
