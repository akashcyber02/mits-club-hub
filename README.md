# 🏫 MITS Club Hub

> **A centralized information and discovery platform for all student clubs at Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior.**

---

## 🎯 Project Overview
**MITS Club Hub** bridges the gap between students and campus student organizations. It provides a single verified portal where students can explore clubs, understand eligibility, view upcoming workshops & events, and find official coordinator contacts.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- 🔍 **Category Search & Filter:** Easily find clubs in Technical, Cultural, Sports, Social, and Innovation domains.
- 🌐 **Bilingual Support:** Available in both **English & Hindi**.
- 📋 **Verified Club Profiles:** Mission, establishment year, eligibility, and perks of joining.
- 📅 **Events & Announcements:** Live updates on club recruitments, hackathons, and workshops.
- 📞 **Verified Contacts:** Official faculty & student coordinator contact channels.
